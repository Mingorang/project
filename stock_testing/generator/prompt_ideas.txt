### IDEA-427 :: Volatility targeting for position size
phase: 4 | effort: M | tags: sizing
why: Fixed position sizes mean your risk swings with the market, and volatility is at a 3rd percentile today.
do:  size = min(target_vol / forecast_vol, max_leverage). Note this levers up in calm markets, so the cap is a real risk control.

### IDEA-428 :: Half-Kelly, not full Kelly
phase: 4 | effort: S | tags: sizing,risk
why: Full Kelly is optimal only with perfectly known probabilities, which you never have.
do:  Multiply the Kelly fraction by 0.5 or less, and document the choice.

### IDEA-429 :: Hard leverage cap independent of the model
phase: 4 | effort: S | tags: sizing,risk,critical
why: A model error that doubles position size is survivable; one that multiplies it tenfold is not.
do:  An absolute cap applied after all sizing logic, as a last line of defence.

### IDEA-430 :: Position limits per instrument
phase: 4 | effort: S | tags: sizing,risk
why: Concentration risk is how a single bad name ends a portfolio.
do:  Cap any single position at a few percent of gross exposure.

### IDEA-431 :: Sector exposure limits
phase: 4 | effort: M | tags: sizing,risk
why: An unconstrained cross-sectional model will happily go 60 percent long one sector.
do:  Neutralise or cap sector exposure, and report realised sector tilts.

### IDEA-432 :: Correlation-aware sizing
phase: 4 | effort: M | tags: sizing,risk,critical
why: Measured, GBP/USD and EUR/USD correlate at 0.85, so sizing them independently roughly doubles true exposure.
do:  Size on the covariance matrix, not on individual volatilities.

### IDEA-433 :: Shrinkage covariance estimation
phase: 4 | effort: M | tags: sizing,statistics
why: Sample covariance is badly conditioned and often not positive-definite when instruments outnumber observations.
do:  Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage toward a structured target; verify positive-definiteness before inverting.

### IDEA-434 :: Turnover penalty inside the optimiser
phase: 4 | effort: M | tags: sizing,costs
why: Optimising expected return alone produces a portfolio that churns itself to death.
do:  Add a transaction-cost term to the objective so the optimiser trades only when the edge exceeds the cost.

### IDEA-435 :: No-trade band
phase: 4 | effort: S | tags: sizing,costs
why: Rebalancing to a target every day pays the spread for trivial adjustments.
do:  Only trade when the gap between current and target weight exceeds a threshold.

### IDEA-436 :: Trade scheduling across the day
phase: 5 | effort: M | tags: costs,execution
why: Executing everything at the open pays the widest spread of the session.
do:  Spread execution across a window; model the resulting slippage honestly.

### IDEA-437 :: Stop-loss discipline, evaluated empirically
phase: 4 | effort: M | tags: risk,sizing
why: Stops reduce drawdown and also cut winners; whether they help is an empirical question, not a belief.
do:  Backtest with and without, at several volatility multiples, and report both.

### IDEA-438 :: Maximum drawdown circuit breaker
phase: 5 | effort: S | tags: risk,production
why: A model that has stopped working needs to be stopped before it does serious damage.
do:  Halt trading automatically at a pre-set drawdown; require a manual, documented restart.

### IDEA-439 :: Report Calmar alongside Sharpe
phase: 4 | effort: S | tags: reporting,risk
why: Sharpe 1.0 with a 40 percent drawdown is unusable in practice regardless of the Sharpe.
do:  Calmar = annual return divided by maximum drawdown, in every results table.

### IDEA-440 :: Report the worst month and worst quarter
phase: 4 | effort: S | tags: reporting,risk
why: Investors and examiners both ask this immediately, and it is rarely in the table.
do:  Add worst month, worst quarter and longest drawdown duration as standard rows.

### IDEA-441 :: Underwater curve plot
phase: 4 | effort: S | tags: reporting,risk
why: The drawdown path communicates risk far better than a single maximum figure.
do:  Plot the drawdown series over the whole backtest alongside the equity curve.

### IDEA-442 :: Long-short balance reporting
phase: 4 | effort: S | tags: reporting,panel
why: A nominally market-neutral strategy that runs 70 percent net long is a directional bet in disguise.
do:  Report gross and net exposure over time, with a target band.

### IDEA-443 :: Expected shortfall, not only value at risk
phase: 4 | effort: M | tags: risk,statistics
why: VaR says nothing about how bad the tail is beyond the threshold, which is the part that matters.
do:  Report conditional VaR at 95 and 99 percent, computed empirically rather than from a normal assumption.

### IDEA-444 :: Factor exposure attribution
phase: 4 | effort: M | tags: reporting,panel
why: If your returns are explained by momentum exposure, you have rebuilt momentum expensively.
do:  Regress strategy returns on standard factors; report the residual alpha and its t-statistic.

### IDEA-445 :: Paper-trade before anything else
