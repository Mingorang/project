Analysis of 300 synthetic OHLC runs
=====================================

Input: 300 matched CSV/PNG pairs; 13500 bars.
Indicators: SMA(5,12); rolling 95% t-interval for the mean of the prior 12 closes; Bollinger(20,2 SD); RSI(14, Wilder); MACD(12,26,9); ATR(14, Wilder).
Trade screen: 5/12 SMA crossover; entry at next bar open; stop at signal-bar low/high; target 2R; if stop and target both touch in one candle, stop is assumed first. No fees, slippage, or position sizing. Best trade is selected retrospectively from future outcomes and is not a recommendation or predictive signal.
Returns and return-based ratios are only calculated for positive prior closes; ratio results are descriptive per-bar values, unannualized, with zero risk-free rate. A 95% rolling t-interval describes the recent 12-close mean and is not a forecast; serial correlation may weaken nominal coverage.
No volume column is present, so volume-weighted or volume-confirmed indicators such as VWAP/OBV are omitted.
